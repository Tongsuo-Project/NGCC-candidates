gen_rounding.py -- CompressY round-half-up magic reciprocals
Exhaustively verified exact for v in (-2^20, 2^20) = (-1048576,1048576).
Kernel: q = (2*v*RECIP + ROUND_BIAS) >> SHIFT;  res = q - ROUND_K
        == round_half_up(v, alpha) = floor((2v+alpha)/(2 alpha))
Shift-invariant (ties toward +inf): restores the CompressY
translation identity CompressY(y+alpha*x)=CompressY(y)+x for all alpha.
