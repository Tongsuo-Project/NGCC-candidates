gen_bounds.py -- SHUTTLE exact integer squared-norm thresholds

Inclusive-gate rounding rule:
  BK_SQ     = floor(B_k^2)    (KeyGen upper, '<='  on B_k)
  BK_LOW_SQ = ceil(B_k'^2)    (KeyGen lower, '>='  on B_k')
  BV_SQ     = floor(B_v^2)    (Sign+Verify, '<='  on B_v, SHARED)
